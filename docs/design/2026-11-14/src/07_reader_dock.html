RENTAL AGREEMENT

This agreement is made on the 1st day of November 2026 between the Owner and the Tenant, for the premises described in Schedule A, on the terms set out below.

1. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of ₹18,000 on or before the 5th day of each calendar month, by bank transfer to the account notified by the Owner.

2. A refundable security deposit of ₹54,000 has been received by the Owner and shall be returned within thirty days of the Tenant vacating the premises.

3. The term of this agreement is eleven months, renewable by mutual written consent of both parties.

4. The Tenant shall not sub-let the premises or any part thereof without prior written permission.

5. Electricity and water charges shall be borne by the Tenant as per actual meter readings.
