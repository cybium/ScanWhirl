## What the competitors actually sell

### Genius Scan
The cleanest split: scanning is free. The paid tier sells **text, archival format, security and cloud workflow**. The comparison matrix lets a user understand exactly what Ultra adds.

### Adobe Scan
The broadest document-finishing pack: **Magic eraser, edit text, high-speed capture, Office export, combine, compress, password, 100-page OCR and 20 GB cloud storage**. Price is hidden behind a plan/web step, which is a friction point.

### SwiftScan
The most aggressive “everything” bundle: **unlimited scanning, OCR, signature, cloud, AI, editing**, at ₹2,350/year or ₹470/month. Free is deliberately unpleasant: watermark, ads, gated filters.

### vFlat
Sells **scan cleanup for books**: finger removal, flattening, annotation removal, OCR and Word output. At ₹2,999/year it is the most expensive purely local-sounding tier in this set.

### Notebloc
Sells **ad removal only**. Three price cards and a “Continue with ads” exit. The lifetime option is the most user-friendly offer in the set.

### Lufick
Sells **ad removal, themes and support**, with a vague “Premium Features” line. It does not articulate a document workflow. The Pro page looks like an ad, not a product.

### TapScanner / Tiny / ACE / iScanner
In this walk, either the price was hidden behind another step or the onboarding was too aggressive to reach a reliable price. They are not safe benchmarks for a fair offer.

The India price band for a credible scanner is roughly **₹750–₹4,250/year**. The low end is ad removal; the high end is OCR/security/cloud/AI. A purely offline app cannot justify the top of that range with server costs, but it can justify a one-time “power tools” purchase.

## Recommendation for ScanWhirl

Do not copy a recurring plan until there is a real paid value pack and a working purchase flow. The current free tier already contains the features competitors charge for: OCR, search, merge, compress. The current paid tier contains only Lock and Vault.

### Suggested structure

**Free**
- Scan and import.
- Basic OCR and in-app search.
- Page edit, merge, compress, resize.
- Basic PDF share/export.
- No ads, no watermark, no account.

**Pro — “Power Pack”**
- Advanced OCR: language packs, layout/table-aware text, Word/Excel/TXT export.
- Searchable PDF and PDF/A controls.
- Signature, annotation, secure redaction.
- ID-card front/back and book-spread split/dewarp.
- Batch page operations and naming templates/default folder.
- Folders/tags, app lock, encrypted Vault, PDF password protection.
- Local auto-export/shortcuts.

**Price**
- **Recommended one-time founder price: ₹1,499** (regular ₹1,999).
- Optional annual maintenance/updates: **₹599–₹899/year**, only if it buys ongoing OCR language packs or a real service.
- Do not use weekly pricing, forced trials, ads, watermarks or a “PRO” badge before billing exists.

Why one-time: the app is local-only, has no per-user server cost, and competes against Notebloc’s ₹1,350 lifetime and Lufick’s ₹799/year. A lifetime purchase is also a strong trust signal for an app whose pitch is “your scans stay on your device.” If a subscription is chosen, it must buy continuous value, not gate a static local feature.
