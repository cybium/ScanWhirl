## What the matrix reveals

- **Home is the differentiator.** The strongest libraries are not just lists: Adobe has Home/Files plus per-document quick actions; ACE lets users choose a Home tool grid; TapScanner has scan/gallery/import plus a tool shelf; Lufick separates Home, PDF Tools and ID photo. ScanWhirl puts all document tools in a bottom sheet.
- **OCR is commonly paid.** Genius, SwiftScan and vFlat gate it. Adobe rations it and sells 100-page OCR. ScanWhirl giving OCR away for free is a genuine advantage, but it also removes a conventional Pro selling point. Pro has to sell something more advanced.
- **Folders/tags are standard.** Genius, Swift, Notebloc, Lufick and Adobe (folders) expose organisation beyond categories. ScanWhirl has only Study/Receipts/IDs/Forms/Other.
- **Security is usually a paid add-on, but visible.** Genius sells password/PDF-A; Adobe sells password; Swift sells cloud/security. ScanWhirl’s lock is hidden and Vault is broken.
- **No competitor is both feature-rich and clean.** Adobe and Swift are rich but cloud/ad-heavy; Genius is clean but OCR paid; Notebloc is clean but shallow; ACE/TapScanner are broad but ad-driven.
- **The offline gap is real.** None of the top installed apps is fully offline with no ads/accounts and rich document tools. ScanWhirl can own that position if it fixes trust and adds a coherent Power Pack.
