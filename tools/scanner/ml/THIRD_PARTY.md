Transitive packages installed by those wheels (filelock, fsspec, jinja2,
MarkupSafe, mpmath, networkx, sympy, typing-extensions, setuptools,
flatbuffers, protobuf, packaging, ml_dtypes) are copied build-time
dependencies only: the venv is a local analysis tool, not shipped inside the
Android app. Their upstream licences are permissive (MIT/BSD/Apache-2.0/
PSF-2.0); no GPL/AGPL component is installed.

## Training-data licences are separate

These licences cover code, not data. The training pool is:
- `commercial/own/` — frames recorded by the owner, declared commercially usable for this work;
- `commercial/synth/` — generated by `synth.py` in this repository, so owned outright;
- third-party candidates (e.g. SmartDoc CC BY 4.0) are NOT downloaded or used here; any such
  addition needs its own recorded licence decision first.

The web stills under `qa/recordings/stills/web-2026-11-19` are third-party images kept for
LOCAL EVALUATION ONLY and are never copied into the training pool, committed, uploaded or shipped.

## Model weights

Training weights and any exported TFLite/ONNX model are NOT committed to this repository. Each
run must record its own licence/provenance and hash under `qa/scannerx-ml/runs/`.
