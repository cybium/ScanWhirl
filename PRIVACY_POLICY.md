# Privacy Policy for ScanWhirl

**Last updated: October 17, 2026**

## Overview

ScanWhirl ("the App") is a document scanning application. This privacy policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

## No data collection

ScanWhirl does **not** collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no cloud sync.

## What happens on your device only

- Documents you scan (photos and PDFs) are processed **entirely on your device**.
- Scanned pages and PDFs are saved only in the App's private local storage on your device.
- Edge detection and document scanning features are provided by Google's ML Kit Document Scanner, which runs on-device via Google Play services. Images are not uploaded to ScanWhirl's servers (ScanWhirl has no servers).
- You can delete any scan at any time from within the App. Deleting removes the files permanently from your device.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** required to capture document pages. Camera access is only used while you are actively scanning.
- **Photos / Media (gallery import):** optional. If you choose to import an image from your gallery to scan, the App reads only the image you select.

## Sharing

When you tap "Share", the App opens Android's standard share sheet so you can send a PDF or page image to an app or person of your choice. ScanWhirl does not send the file anywhere itself.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be included with the App update and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
